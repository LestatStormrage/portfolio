    About me

    Hi! My name is Ivan. I’m originally from Ukraine, live in Germany. I’ve explored different areas in IT and found web development to be the one I enjoy the most.

    Over time, I noticed that many websites are built using page builders and pre-made themes, which often results in slow performance, unnecessary complexity, and limited flexibility. I take a different approach - I build websites from scratch, writing all code manually. This allows me to create fast and secure websites with very custom design.

    I also understand that a large upfront investment in a good website isn’t always ideal or affordable, especially for small businesses. This is why, to make things more flexible, I also offer a subscription-based option. With this model, you pay a fixed monthly fee instead of a large upfront price. This makes it easier to plan and manage, especially for small and growing businesses.

    FAQ

    How long does the website take from start to finish?
    This depends of the complexity of the website and the amount of content. In average, most projects take between 2-6 weeks.
    Why do you write all code by hand instead of using web page builders like the others do?
    First of all, this allows me to create websites with unique designs that are not limited by the capabilities of page builders. Second - by writing all the code by hand I'm making sure that the code is clean and optimized for performance, which is often not the case with page builders. This increases load times and performance. Also, because I'm not relying on builders and there's no need to constantly updage any addons or Wordpress versions, the websites I make are literally impossible to hack - there's just nothing that can be hacked.
    Will you make the design for my website yourself?
    Yes! If you have any ideas, style direction, preferences or other wishes - feel free to share them with me! If you’re not sure how your website should look - that's totally fine! You can leave that to me and I'll share my design ideas with you and we'll discuss them together.
    Will you host my website or do I have to make it myself?
    That’s completely up to you. You can do it yourself or I can take care of everything for you - including setup and maintenance. That is available for 25€ per month.
    Do you charge for desktop and mobile version of the website separately?
    No! All websites I build are fully responsive, meaning they automatically adapt to different screen sizes. Desktop, tablet, mobile - it'll look great on any device!
    I want to sell products through my website. Do you offer E-commerce?
    Unfortunately, I do not offer E-commerce at the moment.
    The subscription plan, how does it work?
    The subscription model is a flexible way to get a professional website without a large upfront cost. You pay a fixed monthly fee, with a minimum contract of 12 months. After that, you can cancel at any time. Since this is a service-based model that includes ongoing support and maintenance, the website remains part of the subscription and is not transferred if the service is cancelled.
    If I choose the subscription model, can I buy out of the subscription later?
    Yes, this option is available anytime after the first 12 months contract. The buyout price is calculated as 50% of the original website cost.
